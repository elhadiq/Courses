Why a hyperbola?Protection

Y |· | · | ·· x × y = k | ···· | ········· +--------------------------- X (never touches an axis)

Buying more of a token makes it steeply pricier — you cannot drain the pool to zero.

Large trades get worse rates (slippage), which discourages draining. Linear pricing would be drainable.
4 / 7

Impermanent lossRisk

If pooled token prices move a lot, your holdings can be worth less than simply holding — even after fees.

Providing liquidity earns fees but carries this subtle, market-driven risk.

Lending marketsOver-collateral

  • Deposit to earn interest; borrow against locked collateral.

required collateral = loan × ratio / 100 loan 100 at 150% → 150 collateral
Fall below the threshold → liquidation, run by incentivized users.

Recap

DeFi replaces intermediaries with contracts. AMMs price trades with x x y = k: the price is y/x and output is (y*dx)/(x+dx). The hyperbola makes pools un-drainable but exposes liquidity providers to impermanent loss. Lending is over-collateralized, with a ratio setting the collateral required and liquidations keeping pools solvent.
