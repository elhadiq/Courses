Recap

Consensus balances safety, finality, and liveness; Ethereum favours liveness via forking and adds finality for safety. Proof of stake secures the chain with staked ether (32 ETH per validator) and intrinsic slashing. Time runs in 12-second slots and 32-slot epochs, and Gasper = LMD-GHOST (fork choice) + Casper FFG (finality).
