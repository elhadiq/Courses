Recap

A ZK proof lets a prover convince a verifier a statement is true, revealing nothing else, satisfying completeness, soundness, and zero knowledge. Because verifying is far cheaper than re-executing, ZK proofs let Ethereum scale (ZK rollups). SNARKs need a trusted setup; STARKs do not.
