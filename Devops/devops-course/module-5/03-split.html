5.3 Terraform vs Ansible — the division of labour

Beginners often ask which one to learn. They solve adjacent problems and are commonly used together.

TerraformAnsible
JobProvisioning: create the things — networks, VMs, databases, DNS, load balancersConfiguration: make an existing machine correct — packages, files, services, users
ModelDeclarative, with persistent stateMostly declarative modules, executed in order, stateless
Knows aboutWhat it created (via state file)Nothing between runs — it inspects each time
TransportProvider APIsSSH (agentless)
Deletingterraform destroy removes what it madeNo concept of teardown

Typical flow: Terraform creates three VMs and outputs their IPs → Ansible connects to those IPs and installs Docker, deploys config, starts services.
