4.10 Designing a pipeline people trust

  • Fast feedback first. Lint before tests, unit before integration. Fail in 30 seconds, not 12 minutes.
  • Deterministic. No test that depends on wall-clock time, network flakiness or execution order. One flaky test poisons the credibility of the whole suite.
  • Build once, promote the artefact. Never rebuild between staging and production — you would be deploying a different artefact than the one you tested.
  • Every merge to main is releasable. If it is not, the pipeline is decoration.
  • Under 10 minutes. Beyond that, people context-switch away and stop watching.
