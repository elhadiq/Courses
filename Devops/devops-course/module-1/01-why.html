1.1 Why the terminal, still

Servers do not have a desktop. A production machine typically runs no graphical layer at all — it would consume memory and add attack surface for no benefit. When you connect to one, you get a text prompt and nothing else.

But the deeper reason is reproducibility. A sequence of clicks cannot be committed to Git, reviewed by a colleague, or replayed on a hundred machines. A sequence of commands can. Everything in DevOps flows from that one property: if you can type it, you can automate it; if you can only click it, you will do it by hand forever.

Why it exists The shell is a programming language whose primitives are programs. That is unusual and it is the source of its power — you compose small tools you did not write into behaviour nobody anticipated.
