1.10 A workflow a team can actually live with

Trunk-based development with short-lived branches is what most high-performing teams use, and it is what the rest of this course assumes:

  1. main is always deployable. It is protected — nobody pushes to it directly.
  2. Work happens on a branch that lives hours to two days, not weeks. Long branches guarantee painful conflicts.
  3. Open a Pull Request. CI runs automatically (Module 4). A human reviews.
  4. Merge when both CI and the reviewer are green. Delete the branch.

The reason branches must be short-lived is mechanical, not cultural: merge pain grows with the square of divergence. Two branches that each moved a little are easy to reconcile; two that each moved a lot are not.
