7.8 Dashboards worth looking at

Most dashboards fail by showing everything. A useful one answers a specific question fast.

  • Top row: the four golden signals. If those are green, you are probably fine.
  • One dashboard per service, plus one overview across services.
  • Annotate deploys. Half of all incidents correlate with a release; a vertical line at each deploy turns a mystery into an obvious answer.
  • Fewer panels. A dashboard with 40 graphs is one nobody reads under pressure.
