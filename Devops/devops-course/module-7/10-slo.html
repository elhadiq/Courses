7.10 SLIs, SLOs and error budgets

  • SLI — the measurement. "Proportion of requests served in under 300 ms."
  • SLO — your target. "99.5% of requests under 300 ms over 30 days."
  • Error budget — 100% minus the SLO. At 99.5%, you may fail 0.5% of requests: about 3.6 hours per month.

The error budget is what makes this operationally useful rather than a slogan. Budget remaining means you can ship aggressively. Budget exhausted means you stop feature work and spend on reliability. It converts "how fast should we ship?" from an argument into a number.

Why 100% is the wrong target Each additional nine costs roughly an order of magnitude more and buys less. Beyond a point your users cannot perceive the difference — their own network is less reliable than your service. Deliberately choosing a target below 100% is what buys you the freedom to deploy at all.
