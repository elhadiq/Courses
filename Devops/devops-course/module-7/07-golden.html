7.7 The four golden signals

From Google's SRE practice — if you instrument only four things, instrument these:

SignalQuestionPromQL
LatencyHow slow, especially for the unlucky?histogram_quantile(0.95, …)
TrafficHow much demand?sum(rate(http_requests_total[5m]))
ErrorsWhat fraction is failing?rate(…{status=~"5.."}[5m]) / rate(…[5m])
SaturationHow close to a limit?memory used ÷ memory limit, queue depth
Why percentiles instead of averages Average latency hides your worst experiences. If 95% of requests take 50 ms and 5% take 10 seconds, the average is a comfortable 550 ms while one user in twenty is having a terrible time. Always look at p95 and p99 — those are real users, and often your most active ones.
