7.4 Metric types, and the one everyone misuses

TypeBehaviourUse for
CounterOnly increases (resets to 0 on restart)Requests, errors, bytes. Always query with rate().
GaugeGoes up and downMemory in use, queue depth, active connections, temperature.
HistogramCounts observations into bucketsLatency, payload sizes. Enables percentiles.
SummaryClient-side quantilesRarely what you want — cannot be aggregated across instances.
Never graph a counter directly A counter's raw value is a meaningless ever-growing line that resets to zero on restart. What you want is its rate of change: rate(http_requests_total[5m]) gives requests per second, and rate() handles the resets correctly. This is the most common beginner mistake in PromQL.
