Checkpoint

You add a user_email label to a counter. Prometheus starts OOM-killing. Why?
Cardinality is the primary scaling constraint of Prometheus. High-cardinality detail belongs in logs or traces, never in metric labels.
Why alert on p95 latency instead of average latency?
If 95% of requests take 50 ms and 5% take 10 s, the average looks acceptable while one user in twenty is suffering. Tail latency is where real user pain lives.
Which alert should page a human at 3 a.m.?
Page on symptoms users feel, and only when the alert is urgent, actionable and has a runbook. High CPU and routine restarts are normal operation; waking someone for them trains them to ignore the pager.
You should now be able to, without notes:
  • Distinguish monitoring from observability, and metrics from logs from traces.
  • Explain Prometheus's pull model and why a failed scrape is itself a signal.
  • Pick the right metric type and explain why counters are always wrapped in rate().
  • Instrument an app with a counter, a histogram and a gauge without creating a cardinality bomb.
  • Write PromQL for each of the four golden signals.
  • Build a dashboard that answers a question rather than showing everything.
  • Write an alert that is urgent, actionable and symptom-based, with a sensible for:.
  • Explain SLIs, SLOs and error budgets, and why 100% is the wrong target.
