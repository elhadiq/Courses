7.1 Monitoring vs observability

Monitoring answers questions you thought of in advance: is CPU above 80%, is the disk full, is the process alive. Observability is the property of a system that lets you answer questions you did not anticipate, from its outputs alone — "why are only Moroccan users on Android seeing slow checkouts since Tuesday?"

You need both. Monitoring catches known failure modes cheaply. Observability is what you rely on during a novel incident, which by definition is the situation you did not plan for.

Why it exists Deploying frequently is only safe if you can detect a bad deploy quickly. Without observability, continuous deployment is just continuously shipping unverified changes — the pipeline from Module 4 becomes a faster way to break things.
