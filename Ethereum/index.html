Mastering Ethereum — Foundations

The complete seventeen-section course adapted from Mastering Ethereum (2nd edition). Each section has a reading page, a projected presentation, auto-graded Python exercises, and a comprehension quiz.

Work through the sections in order. Click a section title to open its reading page.

# Section You will learn
1 What Is Ethereum? The world computer, Turing completeness, gas, development stages.
2 Ethereum Basics Ether units, wallets and keys, EOAs vs contracts, the faucet.
3 Ethereum Nodes Nodes, execution vs consensus clients, syncing, JSON-RPC.
4 Cryptography Keys, one-way functions, hashes, addresses, ERC-55 checksums.
5 Wallets Seeded HD wallets, BIP-39 mnemonics, BIP-44 paths, hardened keys.
6 Transactions Transaction types, the nonce, EIP-1559 gas fees, value and data.
7 Smart Contracts and Solidity Contract life cycle, Solidity data types, atomic execution.
8 Smart Contracts and Vyper Vyper's philosophy, decorators, safe conversion, overflow protection.
9 Smart Contract Security Reentrancy, the DAO hack, checks-effects-interactions, defenses.
10 Tokens ERC-20, approve/transferFrom, decimals, ERC-721 and ERC-1155.
11 Oracles Why oracles exist, three design patterns, decentralized aggregation.
12 Decentralized Applications The DApp stack, censorship resistance, IPFS, Merkle roots.
13 Decentralized Finance AMMs, the constant-product formula, over-collateralized lending.
14 The Ethereum Virtual Machine Stack machine, the four data areas, gas metering.
15 Consensus Safety/finality/liveness, proof of stake, Gasper.
16 Scaling Ethereum The trilemma, rollups, optimistic vs ZK.
17 Zero-Knowledge Proofs Prover/verifier, the three properties, SNARKs vs STARKs.

What is in each section folder

  • resource.html — the reading lesson (objectives, explanations, figures, glossary, self-check).
  • presentation.html — a projected slideshow (arrow keys / space to navigate).
  • exercises.xml — three auto-graded Python (CodeRunner) exercises.
  • comprehension.xml — six standard quiz questions (multiple choice, true/false, numerical).
